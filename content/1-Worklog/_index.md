### Overview of the Internship Journey

This worklog documents my step-by-step progress and technical growth during the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship program. 

I completed this worklog over an intensive **9-week period**, consistently dedicating time each week to research, design, and implement a complete cloud-native data pipeline. The core objective of my internship was to build a **Local AQI (Air Quality Index) Forecasting System** from scratch, leveraging various AWS services.

Throughout these 9 weeks, I transitioned through three major technical phases:
1. **Data Ingestion (Edge to Cloud):** Simulating IoT telemetry data and securely routing it to the AWS Data Lake.
2. **Data Engineering:** Using serverless ETL pipelines to clean, transform, and analyze the raw data.
3. **Machine Learning:** Training and deploying time-series forecasting models to predict future air quality trends.
