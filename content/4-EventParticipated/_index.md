During my internship, I participated in two professional events. Each one was a memorable experience that provided new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with wonderful moments connecting with industry experts.

### [Event 1: DevOps Career Path Sharing](4.1-Event1/) 
&emsp;**Event Name:** DevOps Career Path Sharing: From IT Helpdesk to Enterprise DevOps  
&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00 - 11:30, June 6, 2026  
&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Event Description:**  
This insightful session was led by a seasoned IT professional who detailed their inspiring transition from traditional IT Helpdesk support to a Senior DevOps Engineer role for large-scale enterprises. The presentation covered essential topics such as cultivating the right operational mindset, mastering scripting languages (Bash/Python), and exploring the core DevOps ecosystem, including CI/CD pipelines, Docker, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC).

&emsp;**Outcomes & Value Gained:**  
I gained a clear and actionable understanding of the DevOps career roadmap, along with the critical soft skills required to succeed in this field. As someone currently focused on machine learning, this event motivated me to broaden my perspective beyond just model training, encouraging me to consider the full lifecycle and operationalization (MLOps) of AI/ML systems on AWS.

### [Event 2: Inside The Exam: AWS Cloud Practitioner](4.2-Event2/) 
&emsp;**Event Name:** Inside The Exam: AWS Cloud Practitioner - A Strategic Roadmap  
&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00 - 11:30, June 13, 2026  
&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Event Description:**  
Presented by speaker Ngo Le Tan Huy[cite: 1], this workshop provided a comprehensive roadmap for conquering the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam[cite: 1]. The session broke down the exam's four main domains: Cloud Concepts, Security and Compliance, Cloud Technology and Services, and Billing, Pricing, and Support[cite: 1]. It also covered foundational cloud concepts such as the Shared Responsibility Model and the AWS Well-Architected Framework[cite: 1]. Beyond technical knowledge, the speaker shared practical preparation strategies, including "Map Keyword Thinking," the importance of reviewing mock exam mistakes, and utilizing AWS Free Tier for hands-on practice[cite: 1].

&emsp;**Outcomes & Value Gained:**  
I acquired a highly strategic approach to tackling the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification, specifically learning how to associate real-world use cases with specific AWS services using keyword mapping (e.g., associating "Decouple/Microservices" with SQS)[cite: 1]. Additionally, I picked up valuable test-taking tips, such as the elimination technique and avoiding overthinking complex configurations[cite: 1]. This event thoroughly solidified my foundational AWS knowledge, perfectly complementing my hands-on cloud engineering tasks during the internship.
